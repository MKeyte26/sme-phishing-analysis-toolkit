# sme-phishing-analysis-toolkit
Vendor neutral phishing investigation and incident response toolkit for SME's without dedicated SOC/security teams, covering incident detection, investigation, containment, recovery and reporting

## Overview

Many SMEs do not have access to a Security Operations Centre (SOC), dedicated security professionals, or clearly defined incident response processes. This often results in limited internal capability to detect, investigate, and respond to cyber threats.

With a significant proportion of UK organisations experiencing cyber incidents annually, phishing remains one of the most common initial access vectors. This risk is further increased by automated, non-targeted attacks that continuously scan for vulnerabilities and exploit them at scale.

This toolkit provides a structured, vendor-neutral approach to phishing analysis and incident response, designed for organisations operating without advanced security resources.

- Escalation Guidance  

---

## Purpose

The templates and guidance provided are designed to:

- Assess the risk of account or system compromise  
- Identify and support containment actions (e.g. account isolation, preventing lateral movement)  
- Provide documented evidence for internal stakeholders and external bodies (e.g. ICO)  
- Improve internal security awareness and capability  
- Support escalation to security specialists where required  

---

## Intended Use

This resource is designed to be:

- Vendor-neutral (no dependency on specific tools or platforms)  
- Usable by IT staff or technically competent users  
- Adaptable to different organisational needs and environments
- Adaptable to intenal IT skills/competencies (brief vs in-depth template) 

---

## Notes

This playbook is free to use and can be adapted as required. It is intended to support internal investigations and does not replace professional incident response services for major security incidents.
